# Exact cRTutorial::Init

`0x448da0` is the authored `cRTutorial::Init()` method.

Windows proves the complete 0x1c-byte owner at the cRSubGame tutorial slot,
with `state` at `+0x00` and a borrowed cRSubGame backlink at `+0x0c`. Android
and iOS `Tutorial.o` preserve the same owner and no-argument method with one
body each. VC6 emits `?Init@cRTutorial@@QAEXXZ`; all 12 instructions and both
relocated operands match.

The method clears tutorial state, stores `&GameRoot::subgame`, applies the
known tutorial runtime mask, and disables ambient garbage. The unresolved
member of the composite `0x600000` mask remains unnamed.
